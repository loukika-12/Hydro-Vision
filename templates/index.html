How to interpret each level

Use these categories to prioritize monitoring; verify conditions locally.

Low

Availability estimate is at least 80.

Continue routine monitoring and conservation.

Moderate

Availability estimate is 60 to below 80.

Track water use and review local conditions.

High

Availability estimate is 40 to below 60.

Prioritize conservation and investigate supply risks.

Critical

Availability estimate is below 40.

Escalate local verification and evaluate response plans.

QUICK START

How to use HydroVision

A simple guide to each screen, what to click, and how to interpret the results.

Start here

Choose a region from the Overview page. HydroVision then loads its demo history and requests a water-availability estimate from the prediction model.

Go to overview
01

Overview

See four summary indicators, a historical trend chart, and a plain-language explanation of the latest model estimate.

  • Region selector: changes the region used for predictions and history.
  • Refresh data: reloads the selected region’s records and prediction.
  • Explore cards: jump directly to history, risk guidance, or harvesting sites.
02

Water availability

Review recorded observations in the line chart and table.

  • Hover over a chart point to inspect its date and value.
  • Read rainfall, groundwater depth, reservoir storage, and availability in the table.
  • An empty chart usually means the selected region has no historical rows.
03

Risk analysis

Read the current model category and suggested level of attention.

  • Low: routine monitoring.
  • Moderate: monitor demand and local conditions.
  • High: prioritize conservation and verify conditions.
  • Critical: promptly verify with local authorities.
04

Harvesting sites

Explore mapped demo locations and their suitability scores.

  • Click a marker to see its score and category.
  • Suitability sites shows ranked sample sites.
  • Overview returns the map to its regional view; it is not a live risk heatmap.
05

Early warnings

Review alerts already stored in the project database.

  • Each alert shows its region, risk level, message, and date.
  • No active alerts means no active demo alerts were returned—not that local water is guaranteed safe.
06

Model details

Learn what the prediction model and evaluation metrics mean.

  • MAE: average absolute prediction error.
  • RMSE: prediction error metric that penalizes larger errors more.
  • R²: how well the model explains variation in its evaluation data.
Important: demonstration system

HydroVision uses project demo records, not a verified live sensor network. Predictions, scores, risk categories, and metrics are for demonstration and research. Confirm real-world conditions with official local water authorities before making decisions.
